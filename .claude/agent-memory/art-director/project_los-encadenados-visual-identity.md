The Visual Identity Statement for Los Encadenados was drafted and delivered (2026-05-24).

**One Visual Rule:** "If it does not carry weight, it does not earn light."
A visual element earns prominence/brightness only if it carries narrative or emotional weight. Decoration is cut.

**Three Supporting Principles:**

1. The World Is Muted So That Corruption Can Speak
   - Pillar: Transformacion Moral de Edrick (Pillar 5)
   - 8-12 colors per kingdom, dark earth tones as base, never exceeding mid-value brightness
   - Demonio elements use "wrong" colors — oversaturated, too bright, chromatic edge
   - Edrick's corruption bleeds into his silhouette/animations over the arc
   - Design test: Does this compete for brightness with demonio-world elements? If yes, lower its value.

2. Motion Is Meaning — Stillness Is the Default
   - Pillar: Mundo Hermoso y Vivo (Pillar 3) + Narrativa Cinematografica (Pillar 1)
   - World has resting ambient life (subtle parallax, torch flicker) and signal motion (cat stops, NPC turns head)
   - Pixel art expresses emotion through posture, silhouette, motion curves — not facial detail
   - Animation budget goes to weight-carrying moments: Edrick's hesitation frame, demonio binding, the cat's warning
   - Design test: Is motion ambient (near-imperceptible) or signal (unmissable)? Middle ground = wrong-sized motion.

3. Demonios Break the Visual Grammar of the World
   - Pillar: Demonios como Poder Transformador (Pillar 2)
   - Each demonio violates exactly ONE visual rule of the world in a way specific to its nature
   - Examples: shadow demonio casts light from wrong direction; time demonio has one reversed animation frame; hunger demonio introduces a color that doesn't exist in the kingdom
   - Late game: player sees Edrick breaking the world's grammar — reads as corruption without a tooltip
   - Design test: Which specific rule does this demonio break, and does the break communicate its nature? Generic glow = NOT a grammar break.

**Why pixel art serves this narrative:** Restraint enables imagination. Player fills in ambiguity. 32px wrongness is more threatening than fully rendered horror. Palette constraints are enforced structurally.

**Why:** These decisions were authored to anchor the art bible before any asset production begins. The visual anchor from the game concept ("Everything moves, everything breathes. Beauty emerges from restraint.") was promoted from mantra to decision rule.

**How to apply:** All art bible sections, asset specs, and directorial reviews should be referenced back to "If it does not carry weight, it does not earn light." Any element that cannot be defended against this rule is cut.
