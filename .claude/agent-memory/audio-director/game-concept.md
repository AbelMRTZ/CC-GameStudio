2D narrative-driven medieval RPG. Protagonist Edrick seeks vengeance but becomes corrupted
through demonio-binding. Up to 7 demonios in MVP (6 combat + Gato via voluntary encounter).

**Core audio identity:** Dark, somber, restrained. Binding ceremonies must feel like a burden
being placed on Edrick, not a reward. Intentional silence is a first-class design tool.
The Gato's silence contrasts deliberately with standard binding ceremony audio.

**Why:** Pillars 2 and 5 — demonios as transformative power and Edrick's moral corruption.
The player must feel "I carried something" not "I unlocked something."

**How to apply:** Any audio recommendation that sounds celebratory, victorious, or rewarding
is misaligned with the project's emotional identity. Flag this when reviewing proposals.
Validate all audio moments against "does this feel like weight, not reward?"
