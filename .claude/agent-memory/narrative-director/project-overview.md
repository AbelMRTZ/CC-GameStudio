Game: Demons of Dravaryn
Protagonist: Edrick Velmar — young noble, vengeance-driven, moral descent
Companion: Mysterious cat (secret: it is Edrick's dead brother, resurrected as a demon)
Core narrative arc: "Venganza justificada + descenso moral" — starts just, ends corrupted, but feels NECESSARY
Tone: Morally grey. Victim who chose the wrong path. NOT nihilistic — cathartic ending.
Key twist: The cat is the brother. Revealed in Act 3. Every prior scene = dramatic irony.

5 Pillars:
1. Narrativa cinematográfica (narrative first)
2. Demonios como poder transformador (costs + benefits, binding is SPECIAL)
3. Mundo hermoso y vivo (NPCs remember, world reacts)
4. Sinergia y libertad de construcción
5. Transformación moral de Edrick (grey, empathic, inevitable)

Scope: 9 Reinos (MVP: 3), ~5-7 main NPCs, 7 MVP demons
Cat demon is deferred to a future GDD (not in Foundation schema)

**Why:** Edrick's arc is the spine of the entire game. Every system must serve it.
**How to apply:** Flag any mechanic that lets players circumvent the arc or that fails to represent its texture.
